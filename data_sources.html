Upload Your Mendeley Dataset

If you have the actual Mendeley 38 District BD Dataset CSV file, place it in:

data/mendeley_upload/

The app will automatically detect and use it instead of synthetic data.

Supported file formats:

  • Single district file: Dhaka_2024.csv (one district-year)
  • Combined file: mendeley_38_districts.csv (all districts in one file)

Expected columns (Mendeley schema):

YEAR, MO, DY, Latitude, Longitude, Region, ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN, CLRSKY_SFC_SW_DWN, ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DNI, ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DIFF, T2M, T2MDEW, TS, T2M_MAX, T2M_MIN, QV2M, RH2M, PS, WS2M, WS2M_MAX, WS2M_MIN, GWETTOP, GWETROOT, GWETPROF
